Title: The fold in the map
Date: 2026-11-18 09:00
Slug: the-fold-in-the-map
Category: Showcase
Tags: maps, design
Summary: What a pocket map can tell you before you unfold it.

Fold a street map so that the railway station and the river both show. The museum disappears, along with most of the streets that have names worth remembering. What remains is enough to get you from the platform to the water without opening the whole sheet in the wind.

The crease makes an odd editorial decision. Two places that matter are on the front; everything between them gets to stay. The rest hasn't been erased. It just takes a little more effort to reach.

Try the fold for the return journey. This time the useful landmark isn't the station but the footbridge: miss it and the tracks will be on the wrong side of a fence. The map hasn't changed, but a different strip of paper deserves to be on top.

A homepage has a similar job. A reader arriving on the front page might want the newest piece. Someone arriving at its last sentence needs a way back to the other writing. The little “All posts” link at the foot of this page is the return fold.
